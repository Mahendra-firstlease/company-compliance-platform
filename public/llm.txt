# Compliance Portal India

> Comprehensive statutory corporate filings, corporate governance, Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) incorporation, taxation, and business compliance platform in India.

Compliance Portal India is a digital legal and corporate compliance advisory platform. We assist startups, micro, small & medium enterprises (MSMEs), and corporate entities across India in fulfilling their statutory registration, licensing, taxation, and annual ROC filing obligations. Our network includes practicing Chartered Accountants (CAs), Company Secretaries (CSs), and corporate advocates.

## Core Statutory Services & Registrations

- [Private Limited Company](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/private-limited-company): Complete incorporation assistance under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), including DSC, DIN, SPICe+ Part A & B filing, PAN, TAN, and Certificate of Incorporation.
- [Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/llp-registration): LLP incorporation under the LLP Act 2008, including Name Approval (RUN-LLP), FiLLiP filing, and Drafting of the LLP Agreement.
- [One Person Company (OPC)](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/opc-registration): Single-promoter corporate structure with limited liability protections and corporate legal identity.
- [Section 8 Company](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/section-8-company): Non-profit company registration under the Companies Act 2013 for charitable, educational, social, and research objectives.
- [GST Registration & Filing](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/gst-registration): New Goods and Services Tax registration (ARN generation), GSTIN issuance, and monthly/quarterly GSTR-1, GSTR-3B compliance filing.
- [Trademark Registration](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/trademark-registration): Intellectual property protection, TM application filing under Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (CGPDTM), objection response, and brand protection.
- [PAN Card Services](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/pan-card-services): New Permanent Account Number application and correction assistance under the Income Tax Department of India.
- [Udyam / MSME Registration](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/msme-registration): Priority sector lending eligibility, government tender concessions, and MSME subsidy benefits under the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises.
- [Startup India DPIIT Recognition](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/startup-india): Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade recognition for tax exemptions (Section 80-IAC) and angel tax relief.
- [ISO 9001:2015 Certification](https://complianceportalindia.com/services/iso-certification): Quality Management Systems auditing, gap analysis, and global accreditation assistance.

## Compliance Architecture & Filing Lifecycle

1. **Service Selection & Application Intake**: Structured digital forms tailored to MCA SPICe+, GST, or Trademark requirements.
2. **Document Verification**: Specialist review of identity, business constitution, and address proofs before government portal lodgment.
3. **Government Portal Processing**: Lodgment on official ministry portals (MCA V3, GSTN, IP India, Income Tax) with ARN tracking.
4. **License & Certificate Issuance**: Direct delivery of official government registration certificates, incorporation orders, and tax IDs to the client workspace vault.
